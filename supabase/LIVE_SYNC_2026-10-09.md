## Honest limits
- Coverage was checked by script: every one of the 176 live functions and every live table now has a definition somewhere in the repo.
- **Not replayed on a clean database** (no Postgres in the build sandbox), so a first full replay may surface small ordering issues. Do that replay once on a scratch Supabase project before relying on it.
- Seed/reference data (airports, aircraft models, etc.) is not in migrations; see `supabase/export/README.md`.
- pg_cron jobs created via `cron.schedule` inside migrations (trip-alerts, transfer-address-purge) are included. Other live jobs (eye-tick, aircraft fetch, …) are listed in `docs/EYES_PLAN.md` / the export file; re-check them with `select * from cron.job`.
